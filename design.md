# Section 8: Campus Customs Visual Design

I changed the storefront so it feels less corporate and more interactive and engaging for younger students, mid-career graduate students, and alumni who appreciate vibrancy.

The visual direction borrows the proportions and quiet geometry of Sterling Memorial Library and Yale campus architecture: arched windows, stone-like cool neutrals, vertical lines, framed product imagery, and a strong Yale blue foundation. The autumn treatment adds seasonal atmosphere through cool stone, blue-gray sky, and restrained wine accents while keeping the core palette stable enough to change seasonally later.

The page also combines classical and playful references. Playfair Display gives the headings a Baroque editorial quality, while small starbursts, magical-girl-inspired sparkle marks, soft shadows, and slight card rotation make the shopping experience feel alive. Product hover lift provides an immediate interactive cue without making the page feel noisy.

The design is intentionally built around a stable Yale palette with seasonal layers. Future seasons can swap the seasonal note, background gradients, and accent color while preserving the navigation, product cards, cart, detail view, and chat experience.
