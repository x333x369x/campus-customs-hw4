# Section 9: Ecommerce Usability Improvements

## Working cart

Added a real cart state, an explicit `Cart (n)` navigation control, add-to-cart actions on product cards and detail views, a cart panel, remove actions, and a running total. This helps Campus Customs shoppers understand exactly what they selected and what is currently being added before checkout.

## Clear Yale visual system

Removed the tan accent palette and replaced it with Yale blue, white, and cool slate tones. The updated palette is more consistent with the Yale identity and creates a calmer, more trustworthy shopping environment.

## Product hover feedback

Product cards now lift slightly and gain a soft shadow on hover. This gives shoppers an immediate indication that the item is interactive and makes browsing more inviting without changing the product information.

## Clear cart language

Replaced the unexplained heart-and-zero control with an explicit `Cart (n)` label. The label communicates its purpose and count directly, reducing uncertainty about what a shopper is clicking.
